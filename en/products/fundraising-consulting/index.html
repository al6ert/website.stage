Who is this service for?

For organizations that:

  • Want to organize or rethink their digital fundraising strategy
  • Have actions underway, but no clearly defined journey
  • Need to decide where to focus time and budget
  • Want to connect acquisition, community, and retention
  • Are exploring new routes such as companies, partnerships, or B2B collaborations

Especially designed for foundations, NGOs, and nonprofit organizations that need an outside perspective to turn scattered ideas into a concrete, executable strategy.

How does it work?

  • We arrive at the sessions with our homework done

    Before we start, we ask you to complete a preparation document about your organization, audiences, goals, resources, and so on.

    On our side, we analyze your digital ecosystem in advance and review your context, current actions, campaigns, and channels, so the sessions start from your reality rather than a standard template.

  • Workshop 1 — We bring all the context together

    We share what you have worked on in the document and what we found during our prior analysis.

    We review audiences, goals, messages, channels, barriers, opportunities, and constraints. We do not come to present a closed diagnosis: we work on it and validate it together with your team to understand what really makes sense for your reality.

  • Workshop 2 — We build your journey together

    With all the context in place, we jointly define the organization’s fundraising journey.

    We decide which audiences to prioritize, what we want to achieve with each one, which messages and channels make sense at each stage, and how to connect acquisition, conversion, retention, community, or B2B when relevant.

    We build it together on a visual map that takes shape during the session and adapts to your real execution capacity, so that at any time you can understand the whole journey at a glance.

  • Final map and next steps

    After the sessions, we finish organizing the map we worked on and gather the main conclusions and priorities.

    You will have a visual representation of your journey that is easy to check, share with the team, or print.

    In addition, you will receive a final strategic report that captures and develops all the work done: positioning, priority audiences, channels, opportunities, priorities, and recommended actions. It also includes a phased roadmap and the main tracking indicators, so the strategy does not stay as ideas, and your team has a clear guide to decide what to activate and in what order.

FAQs – Frequently asked questions

Is it a theoretical consultancy?

No. The sessions are designed as working workshops. We analyze your case, discuss decisions, and build the journey together. The goal is not to hand you a list of recommendations, but to leave you with an organized, applicable strategy.

What will we receive at the end?

You will receive a visual map of your fundraising journey, where you can see audiences, stages, messages, channels, and connected actions at a glance.

We will also deliver a final strategic report that develops the conclusions of the consultancy: positioning, priority audiences, opportunities, recommendations, priorities, a phased roadmap, and tracking indicators. That way you will have both a visual overview of the strategy and a reference document to work on it in depth.

Do you work with a fixed methodology?

No. We have a working structure, but the journey is built around your organization, audiences, resources, and constraints. We do not apply a standard model or try to fit you into a predefined journey.

Can we also work on companies or B2B fundraising?

Yes. If it is part of your strategy, we can include companies, sponsorships, partnerships, or collaborations in the same map and analyze how they relate to the community and individual fundraising.

How many sessions are included?

The standard format includes two strategic workshops of about two hours each, plus the prior preparation work.

After the sessions, we finish organizing the visual map we built together and prepare the final strategic report, with the conclusions, priorities, and roadmap that emerged during the consultancy.

Does it include subsequent execution?

No. Fundraising Consulting focuses on strategy, journey definition, and prioritization. Campaigns, automations, CRM, landing pages, creatives, or other implementations can be assessed separately if you later need support to execute them.
